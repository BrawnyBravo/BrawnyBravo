### Hi, I'm BrawnyBravo 👋

I build free tools for games and Home Assistant.

#### Games

**[Forever Class & Race Planner](https://brawnybravo.github.io/forever-calculator/)** — a free, fan-made planner for World of Warcraft: Forever. Pick a faction, role and goal; every class re-ranks, with talents, gear and a damage estimate.

**[WARDOGS Fire Caller](https://brawnybravo.github.io/wardogs-fire-caller/)** — a talking mortar and artillery calculator for WARDOGS, with live squad rooms so your team can share targets.

#### Home Assistant

- **[Roomwise](https://github.com/BrawnyBravo/roomwise)** — Bluetooth room presence that tunes itself.
- **[LocalAppliances](https://github.com/BrawnyBravo/LocalAppliances)** — control Samsung appliances over your home network, with no cloud account.
- **[HA Watcher](https://github.com/BrawnyBravo/ha-watcher)** — an outside watchdog that alerts you when Home Assistant is down, unhealthy or its backups are stale.
- **[SDR add-on](https://github.com/BrawnyBravo/ha-sdr-addon)** — turns an RTL-SDR dongle into a network radio source.
- **[Device Watchdog](https://github.com/BrawnyBravo/ha-device-watchdog)** — a blueprint that power-cycles a device through a smart plug when it stops responding.
